About

I develop PDE-based simulation software combining finite-element methods, GPU programming, and large-scale parallel computing. My CFD research uses MFEM, C++/Python, and CUDA/RAJA, with an emphasis on convergence verification and performance analysis.

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in mechanical engineering at Caltech, expected in 2027. My path ran from Chabot College to UC Berkeley to Caltech, with research appointments at Lawrence Berkeley and Lawrence Livermore National Laboratories along the way.
